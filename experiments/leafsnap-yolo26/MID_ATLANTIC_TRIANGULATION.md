## 5. Method and honest limits

- **Leaf priors.** Dirr `leaf_type` wins; `leaf_shape_lobe_type` is folded into `leaf_shape_lobing`. Dirr has no per-species bark column in this export, so missing fields (bark texture and, where Dirr is silent, margins/lobing) are filled from GoBotany and the row is labelled `dirr+gobotany`. Species absent from both fall back to a single curated regional prior (only *Lagerstroemia indica* in this cohort). Genus-level *Carya* rows use the Dirr genus consensus plus GoBotany bark consensus.
- **Crop triangulation.** The mask is cropped at the LeafSnap foreground bbox (scaled into the 700 px mask space) and measured for components, solidity, significant convexity defects, ellipse fill and aspect ratio. A compound-species crop with solidity > 0.78, <= 1 significant defect, one component and ellipse fill >= 0.40 is `sub_part` (isolated leaflet) and therefore `not_assessable` - never a compound training example.
- **Regional photos.** There is no ground-truth segmentation mask for iNat photos; the mask is an HSV green-subject estimate. Geometry is only trusted when the Stage 2 anatomy framing applies and one green component dominates (>= 45% of green area); otherwise the photo is `context_only`.
- **Stage labels are zero-shot CLIP triage.** Three balanced prompts per stage (`openai/clip-vit-base-patch32`) score the frame; the per-stage mean softmax wins. The colour heuristic (brown dominance -> Stage 1, broad multi-region foliage -> Stage 3, bounded subject -> Stage 2) runs in parallel and its label is stored in `stage_evidence`, so the two independent votes and all raw statistics are auditable. This is a taxon-agnostic triage, not a trained organ classifier; `--no-clip` falls back to the heuristic alone.
- **Tuckaway walk photos are unlabeled** (37 camera frames, no taxon metadata). They contribute stage distribution and pool context but are excluded from species-level counts.
- **Network failures are recorded, not fatal.** Failed downloads appear in `triangulation_summary.json` and the run continues.
