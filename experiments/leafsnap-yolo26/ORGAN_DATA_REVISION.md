## 1. Why the current detector fails leaf-in-context

Current 6-class detector (`dataset.yaml`): `leaf, bud, flower, twig, branch, entire_plant`.

| split | leaf | bud | flower | twig | branch | entire_plant | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| train | 5388 | 31 | 221 | 136 | 152 | 243 | 6171 |
| val | 880 | 10 | 40 | 29 | 23 | 58 | 1040 |
| test | 1186 | 8 | 49 | 26 | 22 | 58 | 1349 |

Structural problems, from the data (not from metrics):

1. **Leaf supervision is 100% LeafSnap.** Every `leaf` box is a mask-foreground
   box on a curated single-leaf photograph (`leaf` = 7454 of 8560 rows, all
   `source=leafsnap`). The detector never sees a leaf that is one of many, in
   a tree, against sky, or occluded. The E3 gate's 0.99 field recall is
   therefore evidence about *curated single-leaf frames*, as ORGAN_GATE.md
   already caveats.
2. **BioImages is the only in-context signal, and it is labelled as the
   wrong thing.** BioImages `leaf`-tagged frames (465 corpus images) are
   **excluded entirely** (`exclusions.json` → `unmapped_organs.leaf = 465`).
   The only in-context frames that reach training are twig/bark/flower/whole-tree.
   The model has never been told "a leaf lives here" inside a tree crown.
3. **`entire_plant` is a scene label, not an organ, and it is actively harmful
   to leaf recall.** It is a full-frame box (`bbox_yolo 0.5 0.5 1.0 1.0`) on
   every whole-tree image. The same photograph contains leaves that are
   **unlabelled** in the BioImages side. This teaches the detector that
   "leaves in a tree crown are background" — the exact failure we want to
   remove. 359 full-frame boxes (`entire_plant`) vs 0 in-crown leaf boxes.
4. **`branch` (`bark`) and `entire_plant` both contain whole-tree context
   with no leaf box**, so the leaf-vs-background boundary is learned from
   negatives that are really positives.

Conclusion: the leaf-in-context defect is a **labelling-policy defect** (unlabelled
leaves inside labelled whole-tree frames), not a capacity defect. Removing
`entire_plant` and labelling in-context leaves fixes the policy.

## 2. Proposed class set

`leaf, bud, flower, twig, branch` — five organ classes. `entire_plant` is removed
from the detector.

Entire-plant / whole-tree becomes **frame metadata**, not a class:

```json
"view": {"composition": "whole_tree | canopy | trunk_up | winter_overall | unspecified",
         "subject_visible": true}
```

Rationale: composition is a property of the photograph, not of a box. It is
needed for the pamphlet/evidence layer and for the "compare to clade" work, but
it must not be a detector class. Map BioImages `whole tree` / `whole tree (or
vine)` / `whole plant` subviews into this field.

Dataset YAML target (new, to be generated — not hand-edited):

```yaml
nc: 5
names: {0: leaf, 1: bud, 2: flower, 3: twig, 4: branch}
```

## 3. BioImages — additional real leaf-in-context images (currently excluded)

Corpus: 1899 images, 85 scenes (`corpus.json`). Currently selected: 1106.

**Excluded leaf-tagged frames: 465** (`organ_tag: leaf`, `organ_category: leaf`).
These are real in-context captures (leaf organ on the plant) that the current
builder drops. They become the in-context leaf source once they carry a leaf box.

Subview distribution of the 465 (from grep of `subview` lines, *approx* —
top subviews observed on `leaf`-tagged records): `whole upper surface`,
`margin of upper + lower surface`, `orientation of petioles`, `showing
orientation on twig`, `as borne on the plant`, `lateral or general close-up`,
`general`, `unspecified`, `immature`. Dominant: **`whole upper surface`,
`margin of upper + lower surface`, `as borne on the plant`** — the last is the
one that matters for in-context leaves.

Per-subview counts for the full 465 are **not enumerated here** (requires the
script; see §7). Target a `leaf_context` subset defined as
`organ_tag=leaf AND subview IN {as borne on the plant, showing orientation on
twig, orientation of petioles, general, lateral or general close-up}`
(in-context) vs `{whole upper surface, margin of upper + lower surface}`
(close-up, keep as `leaf` but **low-priority** — these are leaf-dominant
frames, like LeafSnap).

Individual IDs: `individual_id` is present on all records (`missing_individual_ids = 0`).
Individual IDs for the leaf-tagged frames are carried in the corpus; the
exact list is generated by the script in §7, not hand-copied here, to avoid
transcription error across 465 rows.

**Leaf-in-context usability caveat:** BioImages leaves have no boxes. Two
options, director decision:
- (a) Human/semi-automatic box annotation of leaves in these frames (preferred;
  §6 curation).
- (b) Weak full-frame box for `leaf` — **rejected**, it repeats the
  `entire_plant` error (a full-frame "leaf" box is not a leaf).

Until boxes exist, the 465 are *unannotated* and must be **excluded from
detector training**, not used as negatives (see below).

### Whole-tree BioImages frames — exclude from training, do not use as negatives

Currently `entire_plant` = 359 detector rows, all full-frame (`whole plant`
tag, 360 in corpus, 1 `whole plant` subview `in flower - general view` etc.).

Decision: **exclude all whole-tree / `entire_plant` frames from detector
training.** They are not negatives: they contain unlabelled leaves, and
labelling them as background teaches the failure in §1. They are also not
positives for any class. Keep them only as frame-metadata-tagged evaluation
material for the composition field (not for the organ detector).

Specifically drop from the detector build:
- all rows with `organ_tag = whole plant` (359 manifest rows; 360 corpus incl.
  one held-out 1-off selection difference),
- their `entire_plant` class id 5.

They should **not** appear as `background` images either (YOLO empty-label
negatives), because they contain leaves.

## 5. Proposed class/source-balanced sample counts

Goal: remove `entire_plant`, add in-context leaf supervision, keep leaf from
dominating via source balance. These are **proposals**, not built.

Current source imbalance: `leaf` = 5388 train, all LeafSnap; every other class
≤ 221 train. Proposed per-class train caps (fields in brackets are the source
mix):

| class | train target | source mix | notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| leaf (close-up, LeafSnap) | 2000 | LeafSnap lab 1000 + field 1000 | cap down from 5388; keeps single-leaf anchor |
| leaf (in-context, new) | 1200 | BioImages `leaf_context` 300 (needs boxes) + field/Tuckaway 900 (needs boxes) | **gated on annotation** |
| bud | 31 (keep) | BioImages | data-starved; do not oversample weak boxes |
| flower | 221 (keep) | BioImages | |
| twig | 136 (keep) | BioImages | |
| branch | 152 (keep) | BioImages `bark` | |
| background / clutter (empty label) | 500 | captured hand/litter/sky/structure | **gated on capture** (NEXT_DATA_CURATION §E) |
| isolated leaflet (hard neg, leaf=0?) | 250 | captured | label as **background for `leaf`** only if the gate must not fire on a detached leaflet — director decision |

Total train target ≈ 2000 + 1200 + 31 + 221 + 136 + 152 + 500 ≈ 4240, vs
current 6171 (the 243 `entire_plant` rows are removed; leaf is re-balanced).

Val/test: keep species-disjoint (LeafSnap) and individual-disjoint (BioImages)
splits exactly as `build_datasets.py` already enforces. Do not regenerate the
split seeds — the existing SHA-256 salt (`leafsnap-yolo26-v1-20261029`) must
be preserved so E3 results remain comparable at the individual/species level.

Caveat: `bud` (31 train / 8 test) stays unbalanceable; any bud metric is
reported with n and a noise note (ORGAN_GATE.md).

## 6. Field-like validation groups (exact paths)

Field-like validation must be **site/individual-held-out**, not random:

1. **`val_field_leafsnap`** — the existing LeafSnap field validation rows
   (`source=leafsnap`, `domain=field`, `split=val`), already emitted under
   `scratch/leafsnap-yolo26/detector/images/val/leafsnap_<fileid>.jpg` (330
   field rows in val per counts.json). Keep unchanged.
2. **`val_field_bioimages_incontext`** — BioImages `leaf_context` frames
   (§3), individual-disjoint, under
   `scratch/leafsnap-yolo26/detector/images/val/bio_<imageid>.jpg` **once
   boxed**. Gated on annotation.
3. **`test_tuckaway_holdout`** — Tuckaway field frames, **site held out**
   (never in train), boxed for leaf / flower / branch only. Source RAW set not
   yet located (§4). Gated on locating the frames.
4. **`test_field_leafsnap`** — existing `split=test` field rows (1186), unchanged
   so the E3 number remains comparable.

Do not place Tuckaway frames in `train` until the site-held-out test is fixed;
the three QA frames above share one site and must not be split across
train/val.
