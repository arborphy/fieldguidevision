Verified Exploratory demo
Question
Can a few PCA coordinates keep organ separation or reproduce the unsupervised grouping? Is needing many coordinates a K=20 artefact?
What was actually run

Task A: external organ label (7 classes). Task B: grouping approximation, the model's own K-means IDs for K = 3 / 7 / 20 (circular: same vectors). NSC is supervised; used here to explain.

4. Choice of K + soft clustering

Verified Exploratory demo Not done / blocked
Question
In unsupervised clustering K is a choice: what does each K correspond to? Which photos sit between two groups: two plausible labels, a likely mistake, or just variation within one organ?
What was actually run
K: 3 (shot scales), 7 (organs), 20 (old default); large K left out. K-means on full vectors; agreement with organ / scale / species (AMI, corrected for chance), one-to-one matched accuracy when K equals the number of labels, stability over resamples of individual plants.
Soft membership: resample individual plants 50 times and recluster; the share of runs a photo lands in each group is its membership; max membership <0.8 = ambiguous. The earlier diagonal-GMM result is withdrawn: it gets harder with dimension (22% ambiguous in 2-D, 1.3% in 32-D, table below).
Labels first: each group is described by its organ and scale make-up with a unique name; ambiguous photos are then sorted into 5 categories using the photo's own organ label (explained below).

Ambiguous photos by category (up to 8 each)

same organ — Both candidate groups have the same main organ; the ambiguity is inside one organ (scale, species, background).

both organs plausible — The photo sits in its own organ's group, but the second candidate is another organ. A second organ may be visible.

leaning to the wrong organ — The model leans to a group of another organ; the photo's own organ is only the second candidate.

neither matches — Neither candidate's main organ is the photo's organ: unusual photo, or a label to check.

involves a mixed group — At least one candidate group has no main organ (<50%), so it cannot be read as an organ question.

none

What it supports
K: EfficientNet / DINOv3 match scale best at K=3 and organ at K=7; BioCLIP matches neither at these K (it is organised by species). No K is best for everything, so the whole report is shown for several K.
Soft clustering (EfficientNet K=7): 32% of photos are ambiguous, repeatably (ρ 0.84): both organs plausible 34%, leaning to the wrong organ 23%, neither 26%, same organ 17%. At larger K ambiguity becomes mostly within-organ or mixed-group variation. Ambiguity does not predict species errors. "Both plausible" is a candidate list: whether two organs are really visible needs multi-organ labels (not exported) or a human look.

(2) 2-D keeps 10–14% of 15 nearest neighbours for every model; only BioCLIP reaches the d* rule, at 256-D. (3) The shot-scale axis is PC1 in EfficientNet (ρ=−0.65) and DINOv3 (ρ=−0.51) but only PC3 in BioCLIP (ρ=−0.45; PC1–2 |ρ|≤0.12), consistent with BioCLIP being species-first. (4) NSC sparsifies the organ task only in EfficientNet (22/64 coordinates); in BioCLIP and DINOv3 organ information stays spread over all 64 components. (6) Retrieved centroid neighbours show the cluster's dominant organ 90–91% of the time for DINOv3 / EfficientNet but 50% for BioCLIP, whose clusters are not organ clusters.
