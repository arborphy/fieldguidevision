Operational codebook

Use this decision order

  1. Whole organism or crown visible? Distant.
  2. One organ or fine detail dominates? Close-up.
  3. Connected plant part, but not the whole organism? Mid-range.
  4. Rules conflict or evidence is insufficient? Uncertain.

Scale and organ are separate. A leaf can be close-up, part of a mid-range branch view, or a small element in a distant crown.
